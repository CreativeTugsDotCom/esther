ABOUT ESTHER KATINA, AAPC

Coaching That Starts With the Person — Not the Problem.

Esther Katina, AAPC, is a life coach based in Monroe, New York, focused on helping individuals overcome challenges and empowering women and teens to become what they want to be.

Her coaching work also includes group topics such as positive thinking, understanding the five basic needs, raising emotionally intelligent children, and positive communication including deepening the marital bond.
